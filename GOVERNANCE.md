# Governance

Lineage is a young project with one maintainer: **Hrishikesh Vedantam** ([@Shiki992](https://github.com/Shiki992)).

## How decisions are made

- The maintainer decides on scope, design and what gets merged, after discussion in issues and pull requests.
- Significant technical and product decisions are written down in [`docs/decisions/`](docs/decisions/): the context, the decision, the alternatives and the consequences. Anyone can propose one by opening a pull request with a new note.
- The product design lives in [`docs/BLUEPRINT.md`](docs/BLUEPRINT.md) and the build order in [`docs/PROMPTS.md`](docs/PROMPTS.md). Changes to either go through a pull request.

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular contributors whose work shows good judgement about users' files and plain-language design may be invited to become maintainers. When there is more than one maintainer, this document will be updated to say how they reach decisions together.

## Changes to this document

This document changes by pull request, approved by the maintainer.
