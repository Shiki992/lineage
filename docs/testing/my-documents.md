# Manual test: My documents

The Phase 3B "done when", run by hand: sign in with your Microsoft account, see your tracked resume and deck families, open one, compare two versions, open a sent copy exactly as sent, and walk the family tree. About 15 minutes. Decisions: [0015](../decisions/0015-my-documents.md), [0016](../decisions/0016-phase-3b-dependencies.md).

Part A needs no app registration: it uses an invented test account in a local folder. Part B is the real run, with your Microsoft account and your OneDrive.

## Part A: a test account on this computer

1. Make the test accounts (invented content only):
   ```bash
   cargo xtask accounts apps/family-app/e2e/.accounts
   ```
   This writes `three-families`, `damaged` and `large`. Running it again starts them afresh.
2. Start My documents on the three-family account. In PowerShell:
   ```powershell
   $env:LINEAGE_DEV_STORAGE = "$PWD\apps\family-app\e2e\.accounts\three-families"; pnpm --filter @lineage/family-app dev
   ```
   Without `LINEAGE_DEV_STORAGE` it reads the Word pane's own development folder (`apps/office-addin/.dev-storage`).
3. Open http://localhost:3001.

## Part B: your Microsoft account and OneDrive

### Once

1. The app registration from [microsoft-app-registration.md](../setup/microsoft-app-registration.md), including step 3b: the SPA redirect URI `http://localhost:3001/redirect.html`.
2. `apps/family-app/.env.local` with the same client ID as the add-in and `VITE_LINEAGE_STORAGE=onedrive`.
3. You have tracked at least one resume in Word with OneDrive storage (`docs/testing/word-personal.md`, steps 1 to 10), so there is history in **Apps › Lineage**. A deck family needs PowerPoint (Phase 4): until then, the deck rows and filter are checked in Part A only.

```bash
pnpm --filter @lineage/family-app dev
```
