# .pptx fixtures

Test decks for the PowerPoint engine. Every file here must be one we may redistribute under the project's licence. **Never add real decks or real people's data.** "Lumen Ledger", its founders, its numbers and every investor named in these decks are invented.

## Synthetic fixtures (`synthetic/`)

Generated by `cargo xtask fixtures` from `crates/testkit/src/pptx.rs` (the builder) and `crates/testkit/src/decks.rs` (the decks). The builder never uses the engine. Output is deterministic: entries are written in a fixed order with fixed timestamps, stored uncompressed unless the table says otherwise. CI runs `cargo xtask check-fixtures` to make sure the committed files match the generator exactly.

Every deck has one slide master with five layouts (Title Slide, Title and Content, Two Content, Title Only, Blank), a theme, a notes master, `presProps`, `viewProps` and `tableStyles`, like a deck PowerPoint saves. Every slide carries a `p14:creationId` unless the table says otherwise.

The base deck is eight slides: Title, Problem, Solution, Market, Product (a picture), **Traction** (slide 6: a column chart with its data workbook, and a text box with "$1.2M ARR"), Team (two content) and The ask. Slide ids are 256 to 263; slides 1, 4 and 6 have speaker notes.

## Saved and edited by PowerPoint (`powerpoint/`)

Made by `scripts/powerpoint-resave.ps1` from `synthetic/pitch-deck.pptx` in Microsoft PowerPoint for Windows (Microsoft 365, October 2026) through COM: invented content only, so they may be redistributed. `crates/core/tests/powerpoint_fixtures.rs` checks that the engine finds each edit as made, and nothing PowerPoint renews on every save. Run the script again after changing the synthetic deck.

Found with them: PowerPoint gives every chart series a new `c16:uniqueId` on every save, and a comment adds a `p188:commentRel` extension to its slide. Neither is a change to what the deck shows, and the engine leaves both out.

All 25 synthetic decks, and every deck the engine's merge tests write, open in PowerPoint with its alerts off (a deck that needed repairing would fail to open), and their text matches the engine's (`scripts/powerpoint-smoke.ps1 -Compare`).
