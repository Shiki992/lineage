# Code of Conduct

Lineage adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), as the code of conduct for everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## In short

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good intent.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Critique work, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or personal attacks.
- Never publish anyone's private information, including documents they shared for testing.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 applies, including its enforcement guidelines.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainer, [@Shiki992](https://github.com/Shiki992), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence. For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

The maintainer will review every report and respond as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe: correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban, depending on the impact.
